Breast cancer: KNN versus a tuned Logistic Regression

  
Predicts malignant versus benign from clinical diagnostic features. The focus is on how preprocessing and model configuration change performance, with both models chosen by cross-validation instead of by hand.

  
Notebooks:Notebook on GitHubOpen in ColabRepository

  
Workflow

    

  

    
Preprocessing
  • Missing values found and replaced with the column mean
  • Features and target separated
  • StandardScaler so every feature contributes comparably
  • Separate hold-out set for testing

    
K-Nearest Neighbors
  • Euclidean distance, so scaling matters
  • K chosen by cross-validation, not guessed
  • Small K fits tightly, large K smooths: the bias-variance trade-off

    
Logistic Regression
  • Linear probabilistic classifier
  • GridSearchCV tries every parameter combination
  • RepeatedStratifiedKFold keeps class proportions in each fold and repeats the split

  

  
Evaluation
  • Accuracy
  • Precision
  • Recall
  • F1-score
  • Confusion matrix
  • Classification report

    
Educational implementation, not for clinical diagnosis.






  
Heart disease: an artificial neural network

  
A feedforward network that classifies heart disease from 13 clinical attributes. 303 patient records, 54.5% with the disease, no missing values.

  
Notebooks:Notebook on GitHubOpen in ColabRepository

  

    
Architecture

      
      
Two ReLU hidden layers and a sigmoid output. Adam optimizer, binary cross-entropy, batch size 8, 100 epochs. Inputs standardized, 70/30 split.

    
Training over 100 epochs

      
      
Training accuracy climbs from 49.7% to 92.1% and loss falls from 0.693 to 0.259.

  

  

    
Test results: 91 held-out patients

      
Accuracy85.7%Precision82.7%
Recall91.5%F1-score86.9%

      
Recall is the strongest score: 43 of 47 patients with the disease were caught, 4 missed.

    Autism spectrum disorder: screening-based classification
    
  
    800 records with ten screening-question scores plus demographic fields. About 20% are ASD-positive (161 of 800), so the data is heavily imbalanced and the evaluation accounts for it.
    
  
    Notebooks:Notebook on GitHubOpen in ColabRepository
    
  
    
    
    Cleaning
    • Unknown ethnicity coded "?" (203 records) merged with "others"
    • yes/no turned into 1/0
    • 2 extreme result values removed (800 to 798)
    • ID, age_desc, used_app_before and austim dropped from the features
    
    
    Engineered features
    • sum_score: total of the ten screening scores
    • ageGroup: toddler, kid, teenager, young, senior

    • Models: Logistic Regression, XGBoost, SVM (RBF)

    Findings and methods across the four projects
    
  
    
    
    Preparation beat tuning
    Diabetes. Scaling and outlier filtering lifted test accuracy 4.5 points (70.1% to 74.6%). Everything added in trial 3 gained 0.6 more, while the train-test gap widened to 24.8 points.
    
    
    Disease caught, some false alarms
    Heart. 85.7% accuracy and 91.5% recall on 91 held-out patients, with 4 cases missed. Final-epoch training accuracy of 92.1% is 6.3 points above test.
    
    
    Imbalance and overfitting
    Autism. Only 20% of records are ASD-positive. XGBoost scores 1.00 ROC-AUC on training data but 0.75 on validation; SVM generalizes best at 0.80.
    
    
    Selection by validation
    Breast cancer. K and the Logistic Regression settings are chosen with cross-validation and grid search instead of one lucky split.

    How models are judged
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Predicted positivePredicted negative
        Has it
    TPcaught
    FNmissed
    
        Does not
    FPfalse alarm
    TNcorrectly cleared
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    MetricFormulaWhat it answers
    
    
    Accuracy(TP + TN) / allHow often the model is right overall. Misleading when classes are unbalanced, as in the 500 / 268 diabetes data.
    
    
    PrecisionTP / (TP + FP)Of the patients flagged, how many truly have the condition.
    
    
    RecallTP / (TP + FN)Of the patients who have it, how many were caught. The key screening metric.
    
    
    F1-score2PR / (P + R)One number that balances precision and recall.
    
    
    Confusion matrixTP FP / FN TNWhere the errors fall, split by type.
    
    
    ROC-AUCarea under TPR vs FPRRanking quality across every decision threshold.
